About Me

I am a University of Minnesota student studying computer science and mathematics, with a deep interest in the overlap between technology and the arts. I think some of the most interesting software lives at that intersection, and that is where I would most like to build my career.

I have taken courses on artificial intelligence, algorithms, operating systems, linear algebra, and chaos theory, and I am comfortable working in Python, C, Java, OCaml, SQL, and JavaScript. Outside of class, I have competed in hackathons and been a part of Blockchain Club and Competitive Programming Club, where I have both developed my technical skills and learned from people from all walks of life.

My two projects that I am the most proud of are an NBA Game Predictions Tool and a WCA Scorecard Calculator, both of which are featured in my portfolio alongside the rest of my personal projects. Right now, I am focused on freelance mobile development, building utility apps and deepening my software development skills alongside a more experienced software engineer.
