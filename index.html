About Me

I am a student at the University of Minnesota studying computer science and mathematics, with additional interests in music and philosophy. I have a deep love for learning and growing as a person and as a software developer. I would love to build a career at the intersection of software development and the fine arts and some of my main strengths are my critical thinking, my creative problem solving, and my strong work ethic.

In my studies, I have taken courses in artificial intelligence, algorithms and data structures, program design, user interface, operating systems, linear algebra, chaos theory and dynamic systems, database management systems, and much more. Through these classes, I have built a strong foundation in programming languages such as Python, C, Java, SQL, oCaml, and JavaScript. Outside of my coursework, I have been involved in Blockchain Club, Competitive Programming Club, Rubik's Cube Club, and several Hackathons on campus. Through these clubs, I have developed my technical skills, while meeting and learning from new people from all walks of life.

Outside of school, I have taken on many personal projects to continue to build upon my technical skills and gain some experience in software development. My GitHub is attached to this website with more information on my projects, but the projects I am most proud of are my NBA Game Predictions Tool and my WCA Scorecard Calculator. I am currently doing some mobile development, building some utility apps and gaining software development experience with a more experienced software engineer.
